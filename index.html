FAQs

How do I book?

Send a booking request using the form below. A date is only booked once the 50% non-refundable booking fee is paid and we confirm.

When is the balance due?

5 to 7 working days before the event, or on the event date before coverage begins.

Do packages include raw footage?

Not automatically. Raw footage and raw vertical content are add-ons at GH₵500 each.

Do you travel outside Accra?

Yes. Travel is quoted based on location and may include transport and accommodation.
